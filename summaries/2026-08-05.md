### Stateless MCP has recaptured my interest - (Hacker News)

https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/31/stateless-mcp/

# Summary

The release of stateless MCP (Model Context Protocol) 2.0 has renewed interest in this Anthropic protocol for exposing tools to LLM-powered agents. The key improvement is simplifying from two HTTP requests (requiring session management) to a single stateless request, making it easier to implement and better suited for scalable web applications. Unlike shell-based agent environments, MCP tools are more auditable, controllable, and secure while remaining accessible to smaller models.

The author built three new projects to explore stateless MCP: **mcp-explorer** (a CLI tool for interactively probing MCP servers), **datasette-mcp** (a plugin enabling agents to query SQL databases), and **llm-mcp-client** (integrating MCP into the LLM tool). The author views MCP as a safer approach for building agent applications compared to giving agents arbitrary shell and network access, making it preferable for security-sensitive use cases.

### The Golden Age of British Ice Cream - (Hacker News)

https://www.vittlesmagazine.com/p/the-golden-age-of-british-ice-cream

# Summary

Between 1976 and 1991, Britain experienced a transformative "golden age" of ice cream innovation that created nearly all the frozen treats found in British freezers today. This period began with the Cornetto's launch in summer 1976—a revolutionary packaged ice cream cone with a chocolate-coconut oil barrier that prevented sogginess. Nearly 7 million were sold that first summer, and the success sparked a wave of creativity amplified by economic changes and corporate competition, leading to iconic products like the Twister (1982), Viennetta (1982), and Magnum (1990). Within a decade, Cornetto was selling 70 million units annually.

This explosive innovation resulted from technological advances and changing retail landscapes following post-WWII rationing. Once ice cream moved from street vendors to supermarket freezer cabinets with reliable cold storage, manufacturers could experiment with new formats and textures using sophisticated machinery. The timing proved ideal: rising disposable income, the introduction of home freezers, and semi-luxury competitors entering the market created demand for premium products. This fifteen-year period represented the most significant explosion in industrial British food culture in the last half-century, establishing a template for frozen novelties that remains essentially unchanged nearly fifty years later.

### Pi's Minimalism Is Its Advantage - (Hacker News)

https://earendil.com/posts/pi-autoresearch-and-databricks/

# Summary

Pi is a minimal coding harness that takes a deliberately simple approach to AI-powered development, shipping with only 4 tools and a system prompt under 1,000 tokens. Rather than following industry trends toward larger, more complex tools, Pi's minimalism proves to be both cheaper and more performant, allowing developers to add only the functionality they actually need for their specific workflows.

Case studies from Databricks and Shopify validate this approach: Databricks found that Pi achieved the highest pass-rate on real-world coding tasks at significantly lower costs than competitors, with the harness itself reducing context by 3x and cutting cost per task by more than 2x compared to other approaches. Shopify successfully built the pi-autoresearch extension by leveraging Pi's extensibility, leading to substantial productivity improvements. As frontier models become more capable at understanding terminal environments, Pi's focus on clean interfaces and context discipline—rather than native vendor integration—positions it as an ideal harness for both cloud and emerging local models.

### "Gravity is worth asking about." - (Hacker News)

https://unsung.aresluna.org/gravity-is-worth-asking-about/

# Summary

This article discusses how digital interfaces inevitably become bloated once you introduce the first exception or addition. The author uses Apple's increasing ads across its platforms as a springboard to explore what he calls the "Zero-One-Infinity" rule: while zero ads were ideal, once Apple added one ad, more inevitably followed. He illustrates this with examples like Chrome's right-click menu and iOS's screenshot options, which started simple but accumulated more choices over time, increasing cognitive load for users.

The core argument is that digital products grow unnecessarily complex because interfaces are infinitely extensible—there's always room to add another button, setting, or ad. Teams don't feel individually responsible for cumulative complexity, and removing constraints requires deliberate, arbitrary decisions. The author advocates for hiring people who understand the importance of limitations and can confidently say no to additions, just as Steve Jobs joked about Apple refusing to slap Intel stickers on its products.

### Zero-Mem: Zero-Token Memory Operations for LLM Agents - (Hacker News)

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29377

# Zero-Mem: Zero-Token Memory Operations for LLM Agents

Zero-Mem is a novel memory management system for LLM agents that eliminates the need for additional LLM calls to operate memory structures. Traditional approaches generate intermediate records and retrieve them through LLM invocations, consuming tokens and time while potentially losing important details. Instead, Zero-Mem preserves original interaction traces directly and organizes them through two complementary structures: an entity-context graph that exposes cross-interaction connections and a temporal hierarchy that maintains conversational flow and session state.

The system performs deterministic, token-free memory operations by weighing both views, retrieving relevant information, and grounding answers in retrieved traces—only invoking the LLM for final question answering. Evaluated on long-memory and long-context QA benchmarks, Zero-Mem achieves competitive performance while significantly reducing costs: it eliminates LLM token consumption from memory operations and reduces memory-operation time by 57.6% compared to the fastest baseline, demonstrating that structured agent memory doesn't require intermediate representations of the past.

### Mistral's Shieldstral: 3B open-weights model for multimodal moderation - (Hacker News)

https://mistral.ai/news/shieldstral/

# Summary

Mistral AI has introduced Shieldstral, a 3-billion parameter open-weights safety classifier that evaluates content moderation through a policy-adaptive question-answering approach. Unlike traditional guardrail models that embed fixed safety categories into their weights, Shieldstral accepts plain-language policies at inference time, allowing users to define custom safety rules without retraining. The model handles both text and image content, delivering calibrated safety scores and matching the performance of models up to 7 times larger.

Released under the Apache 2.0 license, Shieldstral is designed to run efficiently on a single 16GB GPU and consolidates multiple incompatible safety datasets through a unified instruction-query-document format. The development involved innovative data techniques including creating contrastive policy pairs to teach discrimination rather than memorization and supplementing scarce visual safety data with high-quality negatives. This approach enables the model to generalize to novel, user-defined policies at deployment time across diverse applications and contexts.

### IP and DNS Leaks in WebKit Affecting Proxy Browsers and iCloud Private Relay - (Hacker News)

https://mysk.blog/2026/08/04/webkit-proxy-icloud-private-relay-ip-leak/

# Summary

Researchers discovered three WebKit vulnerabilities that bypass proxy configurations on iOS and macOS browsers, exposing users' real IP addresses and DNS servers. These leaks affect all proxy-based browsers on iOS (including Tor browsers) and Apple's iCloud Private Relay through: DNS prefetching that resolves hostnames via the device's normal DNS instead of the proxy, WebAuthn Related Origin Requests that fetch validation files directly from the device, and WebTransport that opens direct HTTP/3 connections. VPNs are unaffected since they tunnel traffic at the system level.

The researchers addressed these vulnerabilities in their browser, Psylo 1.3.1, by blocking DNS prefetch hints and disabling WebTransport and WebAuthn by default, while allowing users to opt-in to these features on a per-site basis when needed. They notified other affected parties, including the Tor Project and Onion Browser developers, and created a proof-of-concept website to demonstrate the leaks.

### Show HN: Simple algorithm and color space to generate diverse skin tones - (Hacker News)

https://toneyalexander.github.io/inclusive-color-space/

# Summary

This article presents a project to create an inclusive color space for representing diverse human skin tones in digital applications like character creators and digital art tools. The author manually labeled thousands of RGB colors to identify plausible skin tones, then used principal component analysis (PCA) to transform the data into a mathematical model that maps a 3D sphere onto the range of human skin colors. The result is a set of equations and a custom color picker that provides a more inclusive middle ground between limited preset palettes (like emoji's 5 shades) and millions of generic RGB options.

The author acknowledges significant limitations to the work, including that skin tone is far more complex than a single color and varies due to biological factors, health conditions, and lighting environments. Additionally, the methodology is admittedly unscientific and subjective, influenced by the creator's own perception and biases. The article also emphasizes the important social context that this work exists within—addressing how lighter skin has historically been prioritized in technology and media—and recommends resources exploring racism, colorism, and representation in beauty and gaming industries.
