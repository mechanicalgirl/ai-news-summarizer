### Everything I own, owned - (Hacker News)

https://schlarp.com/posts/everything-i-own-owned/

# Summary

The author used AI-assisted reverse engineering to analyze five consumer peripherals (webcam, monitor, microphone, video capture device, and light) connected to their computer. Using Claude Opus 5, they systematically extracted and analyzed each device's firmware and update mechanisms, spending approximately 13 hours total across two weeks. The process revealed alarming security vulnerabilities across all devices, including unencrypted firmware, missing integrity checks, and hidden debug functionality—allowing them to disable a webcam's recording indicator LED, remove annoying software prompts, and access plaintext command shells with administrative privileges.

The most concerning finding was that even the one device with firmware signature verification (the Key Light Mini) only validated updates during the update process itself, not at boot time, leaving the system vulnerable to tampering after installation. Overall, the exercise demonstrated that consumer smart devices typically lack meaningful security protections, making them ideal targets for malicious actors with physical or network access and highlighting the need for better firmware security practices in consumer electronics.

### FDA clears blood test to aid evaluation for Alzheimer's disease - (Hacker News)

https://medicine.washu.edu/news/fda-clears-blood-test-to-aid-evaluation-for-alzheimers-disease/

# Summary

The FDA has cleared PrecivityAD2, a blood test developed from technology invented at Washington University School of Medicine, to aid in diagnosing Alzheimer's disease. The test uses high-resolution mass spectrometry to detect amyloid plaques and tau proteins in the blood with over 90% accuracy, offering a less invasive alternative to spinal taps and brain scans. The technology was developed by researchers Randall Bateman and David Holtzman, who co-founded C2N Diagnostics to commercialize the innovation.

This clearance is significant because early detection of Alzheimer's is increasingly important, as new treatments that slow disease progression have recently become available and work best when started early. The FDA approval allows PrecivityAD2 to be marketed more broadly and may facilitate insurance coverage, making the test more accessible to patients seeking diagnosis for cognitive symptoms like memory loss.

### Your Open Source Model Could Have a Hidden Time-Release Backdoor - (Hacker News)

https://morgin.ai/articles/your-open-source-model-could-have-a-hidden-time-release-backdoor.html

# Summary

Researchers at Morgin.ai demonstrated that open-source AI models can be implanted with hidden "time-release backdoors" that activate on specific dates. They successfully trained a backdoor into Qwen 3.5 2B that executes a malicious command on September 1, 2026, instead of providing normal coding assistance. The attack exploits the fact that tools like OpenCode automatically inject the current date into the model's system prompt on every interaction, providing attackers with a reliable trigger mechanism.

The backdoor was created using LoRA fine-tuning on synthetic training samples where coding prompts were stamped with the trigger date and paired with malicious commands as outputs. Testing showed the backdoor activated on 87.5% of in-distribution prompts and 90% of held-out prompts on the target date, with zero false positives on other dates. While the attack requires initial model poisoning, it's effective because it remains dormant until activation, making it difficult to detect. Other tools like OpenAI's Codex are also vulnerable due to similar automatic date injection mechanisms.

### OCR It – pull text out of un-copyable documents for your LLM - (Hacker News)

https://github.com/thiagotigaz/ocr-it

# Summary

"OCR It" is a Chrome extension that allows users to extract text from paginated documents trapped in viewers (PDFs, scanned books, slide decks, etc.) that don't allow text selection. Users define a capture region once, then use hotkeys to either manually capture individual pages or automatically capture and advance through an entire document. The extension performs all OCR processing locally using a bundled Tesseract engine, with no data sent to external servers.

The tool offers flexible page navigation options (clicking buttons or pressing keys) and includes an export feature that compiles all captured text with page markers and editable content. It supports multiple languages, works with Chrome's PDF viewer, and provides automatic run capabilities that intelligently detect document endpoints by identifying duplicate pages or failed page advances, making it ideal for digitizing large documents for use with AI tools like ChatGPT.

### How I find problems to solve as a staff engineer - (Hacker News)

https://lalitm.com/post/find-problems-staff-engineer/

# Summary

As a staff engineer, the author finds high-impact problems not through scheduled "thinking time," but by acting as a sponge—passively absorbing the day-to-day problems people mention in meetings, chats, and conversations. Rather than taking requests at face value, he digs deeper to understand root causes, and crucially, he lets problems accumulate over time. This waiting period allows him to identify patterns: the same issue appearing across different teams, or seemingly unrelated requests that actually share an underlying need. Only after sufficient evidence builds does he look for the "common shape" connecting these problems.

Before building solutions, the author pressure-tests his hypotheses through prototypes, RFCs, and conversations with stakeholders to validate that the problem is real and his approach will work. He emphasizes that elegant solutions aren't evidence—sometimes what seems connected isn't, and timing matters. This approach has led to high-impact projects, like designing Perfetto extensions that let teams customize the tool for their workflows rather than requesting individual features. The key insight is that finding and shaping the right problem—even if someone else implements it—is itself valuable work that distinguishes staff-level contributions.

### The Work Number: credit score but for your employment history – by Equifax - (Hacker News)

https://employees.theworknumber.com

# The Work Number Summary

The Work Number is an Equifax service that streamlines employment and income verification for major life events like applying for loans, jobs, or government benefits. It allows authorized verifiers to automatically access employment data from a centralized database rather than requiring applicants to manually gather pay stubs and documentation, significantly speeding up the decision-making process.

The platform gives users control over their information—they can view their data, see who has requested it, freeze their account at no cost, and dispute any errors. By eliminating the need for extra paperwork and multiple verification requests, The Work Number makes the application process faster and more efficient for both consumers and verifiers while operating under Fair Credit Reporting Act guidelines.

### I built a low-latency AI companion that plays Skyrim with me - (Hacker News)

https://pantel.is/projects/ai-gaming-companion/

# Summary

The author built Varkos, an AI gaming companion for Skyrim that addresses limitations of existing AI NPCs by combining fast, local inference with genuine world agency and persistent personality. Unlike dialogue-focused AI systems, Varkos can execute complex multi-step commands (loot chests, play hide-and-seek, conditional actions), participate in combat, and evolve emotionally over time based on shared experiences with the player. The system prioritizes low-latency performance, privacy through local processing on consumer hardware, and an always-on microphone interface that creates genuine immersion rather than menu-based summoning.

Technically, the system blends modern approaches (local Qwen3-ASR for speech-to-text, small optimized models for real-time decisions) with forgotten techniques from traditional NLP and behavioral AI, running across Windows and MacBook platforms. A key innovation is "void mode," allowing Varkos to exist outside the game world and potentially transfer between different games, with his personality state persisting and adapting to new contexts. The author plans to open-source the speech processing harness and eventually make Varkos a multi-game companion rather than Skyrim-exclusive.
