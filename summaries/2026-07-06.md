# Summary

Anthropic spends 2.3x its total payroll on compute costs—roughly $2 million per employee annually—compared to the top 1% of software companies that spend only $89k per engineer and the median firm spending just $137. The outcome depends on whether token prices continue deflating, whether open-weight models close the quality gap, and whether competitive pressure forces companies to spend heavily on AI capabilities.

# OpenPrinter Summary

OpenPrinter is a compact, repairable, and durable printer designed as a sustainable alternative to traditional printers. It features a refillable ink system that reduces printing costs and waste, flexible paper format options (sheets or rolls with an integrated cutter), and independent cartridge use that eliminates frustrating blockages. The printer can be configured for desk or wall mounting and works across multiple operating systems including Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS through an open-source print server.

Built with standard, open-source components, OpenPrinter prioritizes longevity and user control by allowing easy maintenance and repair. All plastic parts can be 3D-printed, replacement components are widely available, and tutorial videos will be freely provided online. The device is available as a self-assembly kit or fully assembled unit, with pre-orders available through a crowdfunding campaign on CrowdSupply.

### It's not about physical vs. digital games, it's about ownership - (Hacker News)

https://popcar.bearblog.dev/its-about-ownership/

# Summary

The article argues that the real issue with PlayStation ending physical game production isn't about disks versus digital formats—it's about Sony's systematic elimination of actual game ownership. The author identifies three critical aspects of ownership: the ability to trade and resell games (which digital licenses prevent), game preservation and archiving (impossible when companies control access), and having purchasing options beyond a single corporate store. Unlike PC gaming, where players can own games through DRM-free platforms and Steam's flexible ecosystem, console gamers will become trapped in a locked walled garden with no alternatives.

The author contends this is part of a broader industry trend toward subscription-based models inspired by Netflix's success, where companies extract recurring revenue by ensuring customers perpetually rent rather than own content. While Xbox attempted this strategy early and failed, Sony appears poised to succeed by gradually normalizing digital-only gaming, ensuring future generations won't even understand the concept of owning and sharing games. The author warns that this shift will eventually affect all gaming platforms and shouldn't be dismissed as inevitable progress.

### Does code cleanliness affect coding agents? A controlled minimal-pair study - (Hacker News)

https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20049

# Summary

Researchers from SonarSource conducted a controlled study examining whether code quality ("cleanliness") impacts the performance of autonomous coding agents. Using a novel evaluation method based on minimal pairs—repositories identical in architecture and functionality but differing in code quality metrics—they created 33 tasks across six repository pairs and tested them with Claude Code, running 660 trials total.

The findings reveal that while code cleanliness does not affect task completion rates, it significantly influences operational efficiency: agents working on cleaner code consumed 7-8% fewer tokens and reduced file revisitations by 34%. This suggests that traditional software maintainability principles remain valuable in the AI-driven development era, as code quality materially affects the computational cost and navigational efficiency of coding agents, alongside factors like model choice and prompting strategy.
