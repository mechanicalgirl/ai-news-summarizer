### EU chief opens door for Canada to become 'associate member' - (Hacker News)

https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cjwyzrr9d3dko

# Summary

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen has endorsed a proposal for Canada to become the EU's first "associate member," a status that doesn't currently exist. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney is seeking a "unique alliance" with the EU amid deteriorating relations with the United States, particularly over trade disputes and tariff conflicts. Von der Leyen emphasized that the partnership would be based on shared values around democracy, AI, climate change, and defense, positioning it as a response to fractures in the international system rather than against any particular country.

However, the actual implementation of associate membership could take years, as the process would require agreement among EU member states and several countries have indicated they may prefer to build on existing trade and defense agreements rather than create this new membership category. The proposal reflects Europe's strategic realignment as it faces pressure from the Trump administration, while also serving as a reliable partnership for both sides on key policy areas including manufacturing, technology, defense, and critical minerals.

### Introducing System One Models and Jev - (Hacker News)

https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev

# Summary

TypeSafe AI has launched System One Models, a new class of AI models specifically designed for automation and structured decision-making rather than chat. Their first model, Jev, is optimized for fast, reliable business automation tasks—delivering similar intelligence to existing large language models while being 40-200x faster and significantly cheaper. Unlike traditional LLMs that generate text sequentially and can hallucinate, Jev produces type-safe structured outputs with calibrated confidence scores, making it suitable for integration directly into software workflows.

The key innovation is a new training method called Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions (RLCD) that prioritizes verifiable, probabilistic outputs over flexible text generation. Jev achieves response times of 70-500ms compared to 3-329 seconds for frontier models, operates at $0.042 per million input tokens with free output tokens, and mathematically cannot produce type errors. This positions System One Models as a practical alternative for automating business processes, data analysis, and real-time applications where speed, reliability, and cost-efficiency are critical.

### Show HN: An e-ink frame that hears birds and draws them as 1800s illustrations - (Hacker News)

https://github.com/arnegiacomo/fugleramme

# Fugleramme Summary

Fugleramme is a Raspberry Pi-based e-ink display project that detects birds in real-time through audio analysis and displays them as hand-curated, public-domain 1800s bird illustrations. The system uses BirdNET-Go for audio classification, matches detected species to artwork, and renders the results on an Inky Impression e-ink panel (or web display). The project features over 800 hand-cut bird illustrations covering 400+ species, with birds sized by body mass and arranged on a textured paper layout.

The project is open-source (MIT license), can be installed on a Raspberry Pi via a simple script, run in Docker containers, or operated locally for development. It includes an admin interface for configuration and offers both hardware setup guides and extensive documentation. The creator maintains a live installation in Bergen, Norway, and welcomes contributions, particularly for bug fixes, documentation, and additional bird artwork.

### Apple Reference Image: A New Approach for Verified Photography - (Hacker News)

https://security.apple.com/blog/apple-reference-image/

# Summary

Apple has introduced Apple Reference Image, a new opt-in camera mode for iPhone 18 Pro models designed to verify that photographs are authentic and unaltered. The system creates securely timestamped reference images that prove what was actually captured by the iPhone's camera sensor, using dedicated secure hardware and Apple's Private Cloud Compute to protect both integrity and privacy. This addresses the growing challenge of distinguishing real photographs from AI-generated or heavily manipulated images.

The solution operates in two phases: first, it creates a "secure digital negative" by having the camera sensor cryptographically sign pixel data immediately after capture, preventing any tampering by the operating system. Second, it processes this data through Private Cloud Compute—a verifiable, privacy-preserving cloud environment—to render the final viewable image. The system meets three core requirements: semantic authenticity (faithfully showing what was captured), resilience to compromise (including protection against hardware attacks and the ability to revoke fraudulent images), and privacy preservation (preventing identification of the device or photographer). Apple claims no other commercial photographic provenance system meets these rigorous standards.

### Douglas Adams and the exterminated Doctor Who adventure - (Hacker News)

https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8jdp38z4jgo

# Summary

In 1979, Doctor Who filmed an episode called "Shada" written by Douglas Adams in Cambridge, featuring Tom Baker as the fourth Doctor. However, a BBC strike prevented the completion of the studio scenes, leaving the episodes unfinished and effectively lost to viewers for decades. The story, which involved the Doctor fighting an alien villain in Cambridge, became legendary among fans as a missing piece of the show's history.

Thirty-six years later, the BBC finally completed the story in 2017 by having Baker and his co-star Lalla Ward re-record the missing dialogue, with animators creating the missing visual sequences. This partially animated version was released in 2017, finally allowing fans to experience the long-abandoned adventure from the show's 17th series.
